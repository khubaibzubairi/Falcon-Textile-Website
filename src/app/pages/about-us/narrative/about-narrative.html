Our Story

Why we exist

"Fashion had become addicted to speed. We wanted to build something that ran at a different pace."

When Sofia Laurent founded MAISON in 2018, the industry conversation was dominated by fast fashion and algorithmic trend cycles. She had spent a decade in luxury production — long enough to understand both the craft that was possible and the shortcuts that were quietly becoming the norm.

MAISON was built as a counterpoint. A studio where the first question was always "should we make this?" rather than "can we make this faster?" Where fabric decisions were made with longevity in mind, and where every brand partner was treated as a long-term collaborator, not a one-season client.

Seven years later, that conviction is still the operating principle. We work with fewer brands than we could. We turn down projects that don't fit. And we still ask the same question before everything we make.
