The founder

Sofia Laurent

Sofia spent over a decade in luxury fashion production before founding MAISON — holding senior roles at two of London's most respected ateliers and overseeing production partnerships across Europe and Japan.

She holds a BA in Fashion Design from Central Saint Martins and an MA in Fashion Business from the London College of Fashion. She sits on the advisory board of the British Fashion Council's sustainability working group.

Her philosophy is straightforward: make fewer things, make them better, and stand behind every single one of them.

"I didn't start MAISON to build a big business. I started it to prove that doing things properly is still possible — and still worth it."

— Sofia Laurent, 2018
